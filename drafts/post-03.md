## Why this post is built this way

| Element | Choice | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **"Ours was too"** | Two-word admission on line three | Immediately removes the "consultant lecturing you" reading. You made the same mistake as the reader. |
| **Caption gives the answers away** | The four arrows appear before the swipe prompt | Counterintuitive but correct: caption-as-teaser gets fewer swipes than caption-as-summary. Giving value in the caption earns the tap, because the reader now trusts there is more. |
| **"Two of those surprises were larger than our entire projected maintenance saving"** | The one line that must survive | This is the sentence that gets quoted when someone reshares. It is a complete argument on its own. |
| **"The physics do not travel"** | Compressed, memorable | A five-word version of the whole post. |
| **Index-if-sensitive guidance** | Built into the brief | A post that gets you into a commercial conversation you shouldn't have had is a net negative regardless of reach. |
| **Slide 6 named "the line we never modelled"** | Curiosity gap held until slide 6 | Carousel completion rate is the metric LinkedIn weights. One unresolved question carried through the deck is what drives it. |
| **CTA on slide 8 includes your handle** | Signature | Carousels are reshared stripped of attribution more than any other format. |
| **DM CTA in caption** | Qualified, commercial | Audience B converts through DMs, not comments. This is the first genuinely commercial CTA in the sequence, and it comes only after two posts of pure value. |
