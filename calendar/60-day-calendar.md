## P06 · Day 11 · P3 · Audience C · Build note

**Title:** We taught Yulisa to escalate before we taught her to answer

**Caption:**
> The first version of Yulisa answered everything. That was the problem.
>
> When a driver asks about a route, a wrong answer costs you time. When a driver asks
> about a [[SAFETY-RELEVANT SCENARIO]], a wrong answer costs you something you cannot
> get back.
>
> So we inverted the build. Before Yulisa could answer a single question, we defined the
> categories she must never answer — and routed every one of them to a named human,
> immediately, with context attached.
>
> Confidence is the default failure mode of these systems. They are built to produce an
> answer, and they will produce one whether or not they should.
>
> Designing the escalation path first is not a limitation on the system. It is the thing
> that makes the system deployable at all.

**Hashtags:** `#AIImplementation` `#AISafety` `#OperationalExcellence` `#AIAssistants`
**Media:** Simple flow diagram — query → classifier → answer / escalate. Hand-drawn or clean vector, not corporate clipart.
